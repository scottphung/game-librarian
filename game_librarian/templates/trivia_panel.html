How Daily Trivia works
  • A new quiz starts every day at {{ reset_label }}: the same five questions for everyone that day.
  • New quizzes come first, in order, one a day. Once all {{ daily.rotation_size }} have been shown, they repeat in the same order until new questions are added, and a repeated quiz says when it was first shown.
  • One point for each correct answer, and no timer. Your first finished score of the day is saved; Play again is practice and never changes it. Your personal best is your best first score.
  • Progress and scores are saved only in this browser on this device (browser local storage), never sent to the server, and erased when this site’s browser data is cleared.
  • Browse Facts shows the facts from earlier quizzes, and today’s five once you finish today’s quiz.
  • Each answer links the source it was checked against. Cover art comes from IGDB.com and belongs to the games’ publishers.
