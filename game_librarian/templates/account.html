What this site keeps for your account
  • Supabase, the site’s sign-in service, keeps your email address and a protected form of your password, and sends the confirmation and password emails. Game Librarian never stores your password.
  • Game Librarian keeps Supabase’s id for you, your email address and the games you save, with their names and when you saved them. Only your own signed-in requests can read or change them.
  • You stay signed in on this browser for up to 30 days, through two cookies that page scripts cannot read. Signing out ends this session.

Delete your account data

Removes your account and its wishlist from this site, then signs you out. A wishlist saved in this browser stays there. Your sign-in at Supabase stays until the site’s owner removes it.
