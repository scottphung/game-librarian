How signing in works
  • You continue with your Google account, through Supabase, the site’s sign-in service. Your first sign-in creates your account; Game Librarian never asks for a password.
  • Made an account with your email address before? Continue with the Google account that uses that same address: it opens the same account, with its saved games.
  • Game Librarian keeps Supabase’s id for you, your email address and the games you save, and nothing else about you.
  • Signing in is optional: without it, the wishlist works in this browser only. A wishlist saved in this browser stays there, and you can add it to your account afterwards.
