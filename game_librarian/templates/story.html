Method

  • First release: the earliest date in the game's Wikipedia infobox, in any region. When the article gives only a month or a year, the story shows only that.
  • Platforms: the platforms of that first release only. Later ports and re-releases are left out, so a game appears once.
  • Roles follow the grouping in Wikipedia's navigation template for the series: main games, and the games listed with them.
  • Waits between main games: whole years, months and days from one main game's first release to the next, counted only when both dates name an exact day. An upcoming game is never counted.
  • Platform families group platforms by maker (Nintendo, PlayStation, Xbox, PC, Mobile).
  • Time since the latest main game runs to the day the facts were checked, {{ story.checked_on|day }}.

Images

  • Cover art is the box or store artwork of the game’s record on IGDB.com, loaded from IGDB’s image server. Each record was checked against the game’s title, first release date and platforms before it was used; the IGDB link beside each game’s Wikipedia link, and the list below, open that record. A cover can show another region’s title or a later edition; such a cover says so. IGDB’s API is free for non-commercial use under the Twitch Developer Services Agreement, with credit to IGDB.com. The artwork belongs to its publisher: it is shown to identify the game, not licensed for reuse.
  • No gameplay screenshots. No source offers one whose content and rights are both known. IGDB’s screenshots are uploaded by its contributors without a stated origin. Steam’s store screenshots exist only for the few games sold on Steam today, show that edition and can show other modes (the first one for Grand Theft Auto V Legacy shows a Grand Theft Auto Online hangar). Wikipedia’s covers and screenshots are non-free files used under fair use for Wikipedia’s own articles.
  • No image is copied, stored or edited. A gray box marks a game without a checked IGDB record, or an image IGDB no longer serves.
